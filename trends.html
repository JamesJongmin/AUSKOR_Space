Global Space Industry Trends 2025
Strategic Updates: Artemis II, Starship Expansion, and KASA Progress
🌙 Lunar & Human Spaceflight
Status of Artemis program and commercial human spaceflight
NASA Artemis II 🔥 MAJOR EVENT
Sep/Oct 2025
Artemis II: First Crewed Lunar Flyby Since Apollo
Following the schedule confirmed in 2024, NASA's Artemis II mission (Commander Reid Wiseman, Pilot Victor Glover, et al.) targeted a September 2025 launch. This 10-day mission around the Moon is testing the Orion spacecraft's life support systems with crew. The success of this mission clears the path for Artemis III (crewed landing), currently facing schedule pressures for 2026/2027.
Private Stations 🏗️ DEVELOPMENT
2025
Vast Haven-1 & Axiom Station Progress
Commercial space station developer Vast targeted late 2025 for the launch of Haven-1, the world's first commercial space station module, aboard a Falcon 9. Axiom Space continues module construction for the ISS attachment. The transition from the ISS (retiring ~2030) to commercial LEO destinations (CLDs) is accelerating.
🚀 Launch Industry Evolution
Heavy lift capabilities and reusable rocket operations
SpaceX Starship ⚡ ACCELERATING
2025
Starship Orbital Operations & Refueling Tests
Building on the Flight 5 booster catch success in Oct 2024, SpaceX has ramped up Starship testing in 2025. Key 2025 milestones focus on ship-to-ship propellant transfer demos (critical for Artemis HLS) and proving full stack reusability. The Boca Chica facility has seen expanded production aimed at achieving a high launch cadence.
Blue Origin 📈 DEBUT
2025
New Glenn Enters Commercial Service
After delays in 2024, Blue Origin's New Glenn heavy-lift rocket targeted its maiden flight for late 2024/early 2025. In 2025, the focus is on fulfilling its backlog, including the ESCAPADE Mars mission (if window alignment permitted) and Project Kuiper deployments. Competition in the heavy-lift sector is officially active.
🌏 National Space Programs
South Korea's KASA and global exploration initiatives
KASA (Korea) 🇰🇷 EXPANSION
Late 2025
KASA Phase 1: KSLV-III & Lunar Lander Projects
Approaching its second year since the May 2024 launch, the Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA) has solidified its organization. 2025 priorities include the detailed design review for KSLV-III (Next Gen Launch Vehicle) and the selection of prime contractors for the 2032 Lunar Lander. The budget for 2025 saw a planned increase to support private sector R&D (Space Pioneer program).
China 🌕 COMPETITOR
2025
Tianwen-2 & Lunar South Pole Prep
China's CNSA targeted 2025 for the Tianwen-2 asteroid sampling mission launch. Preparations continue for the Chang'e-7 lunar south pole mission (scheduled ~2026). The International Lunar Research Station (ILRS) coalition has expanded, intensifying the strategic "Moon Race" with the Artemis Accords nations.
📡 Commercial Services
Satellite connectivity and emerging markets
Direct-to-Device 📱 LIVE
2025
Satellite-to-Cell Commercial Rollout
2025 marks the year of commercial Direct-to-Device (D2D) availability. T-Mobile and SpaceX targeted 2025 for full text coverage. AST SpaceMobile, following its 2024 BlueBird launches, aims to provide initial intermittent broadband. Apple continues to expand its satellite emergency services. The convergence of terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks (NTN) is a key theme of 2025 telecommunications.
