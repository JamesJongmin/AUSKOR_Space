Blue Origin 🔥 SUCCESS
Nov 2025
New Glenn Historic Success: Payload Delivered & Booster Recovered
In a monumental achievement for Blue Origin, the New Glenn heavy-lift vehicle successfully completed its maiden orbital launch this week. Critical Milestone: The first-stage booster successfully landed on the recovery vessel Jacklyn in the Atlantic Ocean. This marks the end of the SpaceX monopoly on reusable heavy lift. The payload (Project Kuiper satellites) was deployed to precise orbit. "The gates to space just got twice as wide," analysts report.

Vast Space's Haven-1 module has arrived at the launch site, preparing for a Falcon 9 launch in coming weeks. SpaceX Starship ⚡ SCALING

Starship Ops: Rapid Reuse & Propellant Transfer
Following the historic booster catch in 2024, SpaceX has conducted multiple Starship flights in 2025. The current focus is ship-to-ship propellant transfer tests in LEO, a prerequisite for lunar missions. With New Glenn now operational, SpaceX is aggressively pushing flight cadence to maintain market dominance, targeting <$500/kg launch costs.
European Space 🇪🇺 RECOVERY
2025
Ariane 6 Ramps Up: 4th Flight Successful
Arianespace has stabilized operations with the 4th successful launch of Ariane 6 this year. While unable to match the reusability economics of US competitors, it has secured crucial sovereign access for Galileo and Copernicus satellites. Europe is now accelerating its "Ariane Next" reusable booster program, aiming for a 2030 debut.

KSLV-III Engine Test & Lunar Lander Partner Selection
The Korea AeroSpace Administration (KASA) announced the successful combustion test of the 100-ton class staging combustion cycle engine for the KSLV-III next-gen launcher.
