🚀 Launch Industry & Major Players
SpaceX dominance, new vehicles debut, and reusability breakthroughs
SpaceX Starship 🔥 HISTORIC
Oct 2024
Starship Flight 5: Successful "Chopstick" Booster Catch
On October 13, 2024, SpaceX achieved a historic milestone with Starship Flight 5, successfully catching the Super Heavy booster back at the launch pad using the "Mechazilla" chopstick arms. This validates the design for rapid reusability. The upper stage splashed down precisely in the Indian Ocean. Flight 6 is preparing for launch, aiming to expand the flight envelope including in-space engine relight tests.
New Launchers 📈 DEBUT
2024
Ariane 6 Debut & Vulcan Centaur Success
Ariane 6 (Europe) finally launched on July 9, 2024, restoring independent European access to space despite a minor anomaly in the final phase. ULA's Vulcan Centaur completed its certification flight 2 (Cert-2) in October 2024, paving the way for National Security missions. Blue Origin's New Glenn is on the pad for its maiden flight expected late 2024.
🛰️ Commercial Space & Exploration
Private spacewalks, lunar missions, and satellite connectivity
Polaris Dawn ✨ MILESTONE
Sep 2024
First Commercial Spacewalk (EVA) Completed
The Polaris Dawn mission (Jared Isaacman et al.) successfully performed the world's first commercial spacewalk in September 2024. The mission also reached the highest Earth orbit since Apollo (1,400 km), testing new EVA suits and Starlink laser communications. This marks a significant step towards private deep space capabilities.
Europa Clipper 🪐 SCIENCE
Oct 2024
NASA Europa Clipper Launches to Jupiter
NASA's massive Europa Clipper probe launched on a Falcon Heavy in October 2024. It will investigate Jupiter's moon Europa for signs of habitability. This flagship mission represents a major triumph for planetary science and commercial heavy lift capability.
Artemis Program 📅 UPDATED
Late 2024
Artemis II Preparation & Viper Cancellation
NASA continues processing for Artemis II (crewed lunar flyby), targeting September 2025 launch. However, budget constraints led to the cancellation of the VIPER lunar rover in mid-2024, highlighting the fiscal challenges even in high-priority programs. Focus remains on ensuring heat shield safety for the Orion capsule.
📡 Connectivity & Market
Starlink growth, D2D emergence, and market consolidation
Direct-to-Device 📱 EMERGING
2024
Starlink & AST SpaceMobile Advance D2D
AST SpaceMobile successfully launched its first five commercial "BlueBird" satellites in September 2024, aiming to provide broadband to standard smartphones. SpaceX continues launching Direct-to-Cell capable Starlink satellites, conducting tests with T-Mobile. The race to connect "unconnected" zones is heating up, with Apple also expanding its satellite roadmap.
Korea Space 🇰🇷 ESTABLISHED
May 2024
KASA (Korea AeroSpace Administration) Official Launch
South Korea officially established KASA (Korea AeroSpace Administration) on May 27, 2024, benchmarking NASA. Headquartered in Sacheon, KASA consolidates space policy and R&D, with a goal to land on the Moon by 2032 and Mars by 2045. The budget is set to expand significantly to support the private space ecosystem and KSLV-III development.
